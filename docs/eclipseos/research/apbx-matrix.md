Compiled from `apbx/*.md` (group-a, group-b, group-c, kt-tweaks) and `apbx-comparison.md` on 2026-11-03. Unchanged cells describe surveyed default behaviour; merged cells describe source instructions and their conditions; "opt" = available as an option; "?" = not checked in the source passes. Marks: ✅ keeps/safe, ⚠️ risky, ❌ breaks/removes for Store, Xbox or security. Per-cell citations live in the group files; nothing here was run. Release-tag verification below supersedes conflicting survey input; resolved conflicts are in the footnotes ([n]). Atlas is repeated in every table as the reference column.

Merged APBX-VER-1..3 on 2026-11-03. [V1](apbx/verify-1.md), [V2](apbx/verify-2.md), and [V3](apbx/verify-3.md) contain the per-cell primary tag URLs, path/line citations and Admiralty grades. A1 describes source declarations/instructions; B2 describes absence within each audit's listed text files. Neither proves runtime compatibility or opaque/downloaded utility behavior. Unchanged cells remain legacy survey input and have not all been re-verified. Long cells intentionally preserve the source and option limits.

Unknown count after merge and `SRC-GRADE-1`: **28 question-bearing area cells**, including 23 Synergy tag-1.7 cells awaiting re-verification, three Privacy+ 0.8.3 cells, and two GamerzOS outcome cells. Three Synergy tag-1.7 cells and the three repeated Atlas ISO-value cells were resolved from primary text. The original 67 assigned question-bearing cells are covered by V1–V3; the count is by cells, not literal punctuation. See [qmark verification 1](qmark-verify-1.md).

## KT-TWEAKS note

KT-TWEAKS-APBX is not a playbook. It is a website plus `public/catalog.json` that re-hosts 9 third-party `.apbx` files: Atlas 0.5.0-hotfix, Atom, FSOS-X, RapidOS, ReviOS, SapphireOS, SynergyOS, Vain and XOS (UltraOS 1.1.1 is a release asset missing from the catalog). Only the Atlas hash was matched to the upstream release; for the others it proves "unchanged since KT uploaded it". Atom, FSOS-X, Vain, XOS and SapphireOS are not columns above (no readable source or out of scope); Atom removes Store, winget and Xbox identity and disables AppXSvc, ClipSVC, wuauserv and BITS. Every catalog playbook with readable source turns VBS off. Its "KT WIRZADE" tool is a closed-origin AME Wizard fork with committed binaries and a private verification API. Verdict (kt-tweaks.md, apbx-comparison): no dependency, no source.
